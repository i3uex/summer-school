About

A meeting point for the next wave of AI-augmented engineering

The Second International Summer School on LLM-based Agents for Software Engineering covers the latest trends related to the application of Artificial Intelligence techniques in software development. Building on the success of the 2025 inaugural edition.

What you'll explore

Three intensive days of lectures, hands-on workshops and discussions led by senior researchers and industry practitioners working at the intersection of LLM-based agents and software engineering.

A panel of high-level speakers with extensive experience in the research lines pursued within the program.
Agents for SE Tasks

Requirements, coding, testing, deployment, and other software engineering activities augmented by LLM-based agents.

Evaluation & Benchmarking

Monitoring and evaluation frameworks for agentic systems in software engineering contexts.

Hybrid Teams

Human-agent collaboration methodologies and frameworks for effective software development.

The school takes place at INTIA, University of Extremadura — in the heart of Cáceres, a city blending Roman, Islamic, Gothic and Renaissance influences.

Discover Cáceres, a magnificent UNESCO World Heritage city founded in 25 BC that seamlessly blends Roman, Islamic, Gothic and Renaissance influences. Its perfectly preserved medieval quarter features imposing city walls, noble houses, and towers that transport visitors back through centuries of Spanish history.

Beyond its rich historical legacy, modern Cáceres thrives as a cultural center, home to the internationally acclaimed Helga de Alvear Museum of Contemporary Art. The city's exceptional culinary scene is crowned by Atrio, a three-Michelin-starred restaurant nestled within the ancient walls.

Surrounded by the enchanting Extremadura landscape, the city proudly preserves its heritage while embracing its future as a candidate for European Capital of Culture 2031.
